Instant Translation

Put your iPhone on the table and talk. Each person sees the other's words in their own language, live. It all happens on the phone, and it keeps working offline.

SupportPrivacy Policy

Support

For help, email sober@duck.com. Include your iPhone model, your iOS version, the two languages you picked, and what happened.

Common fixes

  • It won't start listening. Go to Settings › Privacy & Security and turn on both Microphone and Speech Recognition for Instant Translation.
  • A language needs downloading. The first time you use a language, iOS downloads its translation pack. After that, it works offline.
  • Subscription. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. On a new iPhone, tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen. Subscribing needs an internet connection, but translating after that doesn't.
  • It stopped mid-conversation. Leaving the app ends the session. Keep the app open while you talk. The screen stays awake while it's listening.

Requires iPhone with iOS 26 or later.

Privacy Policy

Instant Translation doesn't collect, send, or sell any data. Your conversations never leave your iPhone.

What the app uses

  • Microphone: so it can hear the conversation. It listens only while a session is running, and it stops when you tap Stop or leave the app.
  • Speech Recognition: so it can turn speech into text. This uses Apple's on-device speech recognition, so the audio stays on your iPhone.

Translation

Translation uses Apple's on-device Translation framework. The app downloads language packs from Apple once, when you ask for a language. After that, translation happens entirely on your iPhone. Spoken playback uses your iPhone's built-in voices.

What's stored

Your past translations and your language choice are saved on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage. They are never uploaded anywhere by the app. To delete your past translations, tap Clear History in Past Translations. Deleting the app deletes everything. Like any app data, they may be included in your own iPhone backup.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, email, or payment details. The app only asks Apple, on your iPhone, whether your subscription is active.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, ads, or tracking of any kind.
  • No servers: the app doesn't send your audio, text, or usage to us or anyone else.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
